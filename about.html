Hi! I'm Lance — a game designer and writer from Los Angeles, California.

I graduated from UC Santa Cruz in 2024 with a BS in Computer Science, Game Design. I worked as the Narrative Lead and Producer on Through the Fog, a third-person rogue-lite exploration game published to Steam in June 2024.

My core strength is collaborating with creative teams to bridge different disciplines in game development and bring ideas to reality with a holistic approach. When I'm not working, I'm usually hiking in the Gorge or experimenting with film photography.
