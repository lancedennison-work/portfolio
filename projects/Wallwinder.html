Wallwinder is a one-button arcade game made with crisp-game-lib. Hold to freeze time: while time is stopped, an aiming bar winds around the ball, and letting go launches the ball in that direction. While time is running, blocks break out of the walls and slide toward the center, and touching anything ends the run.

The catch is that your score only goes up while time is moving, so every pause to line up a safe shot costs you points. The bar also spins faster the longer you hold and reverses direction each time, so a long wait makes it harder to aim.
