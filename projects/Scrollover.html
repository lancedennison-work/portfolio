A small concept prototype for a physical interactive exibit about following color cues. The board is a grid of hidden dots, and hovering over one reveals its color: red when you're far from the hidden goal, shifting to green as you get closer. Revealed dots fade back after a couple of seconds, so you have to remember where you've been. Find the goal to win.
