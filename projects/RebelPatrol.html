Rebel Patrol is my mod of Rocket Patrol, the starter Phaser game from UCSC's CMPM 120, turned into a Star Wars themed two-player shooter. Both players share one keyboard and race to shoot down rebel ships before the clock runs out. Every hit adds time back to the clock, and the Millennium Falcon is a faster target worth more points.

Past the assignment's mod list, I made the rockets steerable after they're fired: the angle drives the velocity through sine and cosine, and rockets speed up the longer they fly. Fly a full loop before you hit a ship and your next score is doubled, stacking with every extra loop until you score or miss. I also redid the art with new ship sprites, an animated rocket, a parallax starfield, a new explosion, and small UI pops for added time and points.
