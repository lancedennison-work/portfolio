An arcade adaptation of 2001: A Space Odyssey in three stages, each its own scene with its own mechanic and each built around a moment from the film. First, survive the arrival of the Obelisk. Then shut down HAL 9000's memory banks one by one while HAL talks its way through its own shutdown. Finally, steer your escape pod into the beyond while dodging obelisks.
