A Journey of Fate is a co-op tabletop card game with a digital boss, made as our final project for ARTG 120 at UCSC. Players fight four bosses in a row: the Traveler, the Beloved, the Brute and the Dragon. This app runs the bosses. Each round the boss announces its next action, the players plan around it and play their Heroes' actions, enter the damage they dealt, and then the boss carries out its move. Boss health scales with the number of players at the table.

The app is only the digital half of the game, so players track their own health and statuses with physical tokens.
