    Hi! I'm Lance.

    I'm a game producer and writer from Los Angeles California. I graduated from the University of California, Santa Cruz in 2024 with a Bachelors of Science in Computer Science, Game Design. I worked as the Narrative Lead and Producer on Through the Fog, a third-person rogue-lite exploration game that was published to Steam in June of 2024.

    My skills encompass writing, game design, team coordnation, administrative skills, interpersonal skills, quality assurance, and production planning. My core strength is collaborating with creative teams to bridge different disciplines in game development, and bring ideas to reality with a holistic approach. I have a passion for the internal structure of game development, working with talented people, and genuinely love each piece that makes a game whole. I have experience working with and alongside other disciplines to manage workflow, schedule deadlines, conduct meetings, playtests, and integrate assets between teams.

    My enthusiasm for game development stems from my love for storytelling and appreciation for meaningful gameplay integration. Outside of my own skills, I have a deep love for art, animation, and great UI and UX design. I love playing most genres of video games but have sunk most of my time in MMOs, RPGs, and rogue-lite/like titles. I also have a passion for TTRPGs and have created several classes and subclasses as personal projects as well as multiple campaigns for me and my friends to play together.
